# Target Venue Candidates

Journal metrics, submission policies, and thematic calls change; re-verify
the current author guidelines page for whichever venue is chosen
immediately before formatting the final manuscript (Phase 6). The notes
below reflect what was found while building this environment and should be
treated as a starting recommendation, not a final decision.

## Primary recommendation: Journal of Cloud Computing: Advances, Systems
and Applications (JoCCASA)

- Publisher: Springer (SpringerOpen), fully open access.
- ISSN: 2192-113X.
- Scope: all aspects of cloud computing — cloud applications, cloud
  systems, and advances toward "the Clouds of the future"; explicitly also
  welcomes work that relates cloud computing to adjacent paradigms.
- **Currently runs an open call/thematic series specifically on "next
  generation cloud computing driven by artificial intelligence"** — this is
  a strong, direct fit for ACDE and worth explicitly writing toward.
- Reported 2022 metrics: 4.0 two-year impact factor, 4.4 five-year impact
  factor, SNIP 1.711, SJR 0.976 (verify current figures before citing them
  in any cover letter — these move year to year).
- Reported 2023 turnaround: median 10 days to first decision, median 116
  days submission-to-accept — comparatively fast for a journal, useful if
  there's a graduation/deadline pressure.
- Article types include standard Research Article as well as a "Software
  article" type, which may be a genuinely good fit for a paper this
  grounded in a real, working codebase — worth checking whether that track
  has different length/format expectations than a standard research article.

## Alternatives worth considering

These are reputable, plausible-fit venues based on general scope — their
current impact factor, review speed, and submission requirements were not
independently re-verified while building this environment, so confirm
before committing:

- **Future Generation Computer Systems** (Elsevier) — broad
  systems/cloud/distributed-computing scope, well-established.
- **IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing** — strong fit topically, IEEE's
  submission/formatting requirements are more rigid (typically IEEE
  two-column format, stricter page limits) — check page limits.
- **Journal of Big Data** (Springer) — a fit if the paper leans more on the
  data-engineering/pipeline side than the agentic-control-systems side.
- **Cluster Computing** (Springer) — systems/distributed-computing scope,
  reasonable fit for a control-plane architecture paper.
- **arXiv preprint** — regardless of final journal target, posting a
  preprint first (as the base paper itself did) is low-risk and establishes
  priority/timestamp early, and costs nothing.
